Refund & Cancellation Policy

One-Time Purchase — No Auto-Renewing Subscriptions

BlinkLens Pro (pro_lifetime_unlock) is a single one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing. There are no recurring monthly or yearly charges to cancel.

1. Standard Google Play Refunds (Within 48 Hours)

If you purchased BlinkLens Pro by mistake or it does not work as expected on your device, you can request an automatic refund directly through Google Play Refund Help or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history within 48 hours of purchase.

2. Developer-Assisted Refunds & Defective Purchases

If more than 48 hours have passed and BlinkLens Pro is defective on your phone or you encountered a billing issue, email nothexaline@gmail.com within 14 days with your Google Play Order Number (starts with GPA.) and your device model. We will review and process eligible refunds via Google Play Console.

3. Regional Statutory Consumer Rights (EU, UK & Turkey)

  • EU & UK Consumers: Digital content unlocked immediately upon purchase is subject to Google Play's pre-purchase digital delivery notice. Nothing in this policy waives your mandatory statutory remedies for faulty or non-conforming digital content.
  • Turkey Consumers (Law No. 6502): Digital content delivered instantly with your prior acknowledgment is governed by Distance Contracts Regulation Art. 15(ğ), while your statutory rights for defective digital goods remain fully protected.

4. Restoring Your Purchase on a New Phone

If you reinstall BlinkLens or switch Android phones using the same Google Play account, you do not need to buy Pro again. Open BlinkLens → tap the upgrade badge → tap Restore Purchase.
