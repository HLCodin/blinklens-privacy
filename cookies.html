Cookie & Mobile Tracking Policy

Zero Website Cookies & Zero Web Analytics

This legal website uses ZERO cookies, ZERO localStorage tracking, ZERO web analytics (no Google Analytics / Meta Pixel), and ZERO third-party embeds. Because no non-essential cookies or trackers are placed by this website, a website cookie consent banner is not required under EU ePrivacy, UK PECR, or Turkey KVKK rules.

1. Mobile Advertising Identifiers in the Android App (Free Tier)

While this website sets no cookies, the Free version of the BlinkLens Android app uses the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP):

  • What AdMob Uses: Device Advertising ID (AAID), approximate IP-based location, and ad-delivery/fraud-prevention tokens.
  • Consent Before Ad Tracking (GDPR / UK / US State Laws): Where required by regional law, BlinkLens requests consent via Google UMP before serving personalized ads.
  • How to Withdraw or Manage Consent:
    1. Inside BlinkLens: open Settings → Manage Ad Privacy Consent (available in regions where UMP applies).
    2. On Android: open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads) to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
    3. Or upgrade once to BlinkLens Pro, which permanently disables and unloads the AdMob SDK.

2. Local On-Device App Storage

BlinkLens stores your app preferences (such as zoom strength, theme, and language) locally in Android DataStore strictly to run the app. This local storage is never used for cross-app tracking or profiling.
