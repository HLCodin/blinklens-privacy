BlinkLens Privacy Policy & Data Safety

On-Device Camera & Accessibility Processing

Zero camera photos, videos, facial geometry, or screen contents ever leave your phone. BlinkLens processes front and rear camera frames strictly in temporary memory (volatile RAM) using an offline bundled ML Kit model to detect intentional blink gestures and magnify printed menus. Frames are discarded in milliseconds and are never recorded, stored, or uploaded.

4. Children's Privacy

BlinkLens is designed as an adult/senior reading accessibility tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information via support email, contact nothexaline@gmail.com for immediate deletion.
